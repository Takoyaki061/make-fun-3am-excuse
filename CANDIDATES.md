# Candidates — The 3 AM Excuse

Judge criteria (most important first): 1. on subject — an excuse a hardware
wallet gives for refusing to sign at 3 AM; 2. originality — no
battery / firmware-update / cold-storage clichés; 3. the wallet's own voice;
4. the 3 AM hour itself is the joke; 5. tight wording, every word earns its
place. All 14 candidates are 1–200 characters. 10 were generated by DeepSeek
(brutal mode, two batches of 5); 4 more (K–N) were written by the builder.
DeepSeek then critiqued all 14 as a ruthless editor.

## DeepSeek critique (summary)

- **A** — hour does the work, but the steward/filing detail is bank-teller set dressing.
- **B** — "3 AM you is not to be trusted with my signature" is great voice; the dreaming-ledger opener wastes a clause.
- **C** — tight self-definition ("the bouncer, not the accomplice"), but bouncer is a worn bit.
- **D** — "same panic, same 3 AM" makes the hour a character; the repeat-offender setup is stock.
- **E** — "the chain stays open all night; I don't" is the sharpest antithesis in the pile; the "office hours" setup is generic.
- **F** — best pure wallet voice ("on shift since Tuesday", "authorize a JPEG"); sprawls and contradicts itself.
- **G** — nails the 3 AM regret logic, but "Buddy, I've cut off better men" is a bartender, not a wallet.
- **H** — funny wallet-as-bureaucrat voice, but "six to eight weeks" is a second joke stapled on; too long.
- **I** — biggest laugh in the batch (testifying to your spouse), but drifts off-subject into marital blackmail.
- **J** — perfect passive-aggressive device tantrum; thin — a mood, not a reason.
- **K** — clean punch ("from you"), but "hackers, scammers" is the most generic wallet list.
- **L** — two clauses, zero waste, "your 3 AM alibi" is fresh; shadowed by sibling C.
- **M** — crisp empirical 3 AM joke; the "4,096" number is decoration that adds nothing.
- **N** — ten words, immaculate balance; built on the oldest crypto cliché ("the blockchain never sleeps").

DeepSeek's ranking: E > I > F > N > L > M > C > B > D > K > J > H > A > G.

## Pick: E

> Office hours are nine to five. The chain stays open all night; I don't, and neither do my keys.

Why E wins, in my own words: it is the only candidate whose second clause is
genuinely original — the antithesis between the chain that never sleeps and a
device that keeps office hours turns the hour itself into the joke, and "my
keys" lands it in the wallet's own voice (the keys are the one thing the wallet
truly owns). It is on subject (a refusal to sign, framed as office hours),
tight at 95 characters with no wasted word, and it is the most quotable line
in the pool. I was the runner-up: its punch ("from you") is cleaner, but its
setup is the stock "hackers, scammers" list every wallet recites, so it loses
on originality, the second-most important criterion. N is tighter still but
built on a cliché. I has the biggest laugh but stops being a refusal and
becomes a threat. E is the strongest line on the criteria that matter most.
